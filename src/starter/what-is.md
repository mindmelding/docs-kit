Product is a [what it is] for [who]. Say it in one sentence, the way you'd say it out loud.

## Why it exists

Two or three sentences on the problem, in the customer's words. What were they doing before, and what went wrong?

## What it does

- **First job.** What the customer gets, not how it works.
- **Second job.** Same shape.
- **Third job.** Keep the list to what exists today.

## Who it's for

Name the people who get the most out of it, and anyone it isn't for yet.
